21_21 DESIGN SIGHT

Two folded steel plates laid on a garden lawn. Tadao Ando and Nikken Sekkei, 2007, after Issey Miyake's idea of a single piece of cloth.

The roof plates run up to 54 m and are 16 mm thick, welded on site into one sheet. One storey sits above ground and one below; about eighty per cent of the building is underground.

The model

Built from a SketchUp model of unknown authorship, registered to the PLATEAU Minato City 3D model (CC BY 4.0), then corrected against official material and public photographs: entrance, roof plate, low wall, ground slope, frosted guard and interior finishes. Dimensions marked as inferred are not measured. The basement and interior partitions are simplified.
