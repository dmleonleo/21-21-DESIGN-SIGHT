21_21 DESIGN SIGHT

Two folded steel plates laid on a garden lawn. Designed by Tadao Ando with Nikken Sekkei and completed in 2007, it grew from Issey Miyake's idea of “a piece of cloth”.

Each roof is a single sheet of 16 mm steel up to 54 m long, welded together on site. One storey sits above ground and one below; about eighty per cent of the building is underground.

Who we are

We are Bella & Leon, a designer couple from Shanghai. We visit 21_21 DESIGN SIGHT often and have long loved Tadao Ando's building. This is the first online experience we have launched, an experiment in finding better ways to experience architecture from afar.

The model

Built from a SketchUp model of unknown authorship, registered to the PLATEAU Minato City 3D model (CC BY 4.0), then checked against official material and public photographs: entrance, roof plate, low wall, ground slope, frosted guard and interior finishes. Some dimensions are inferred rather than measured. The basement and interior partitions are simplified.

Now showing

Four exhibitions, one at a time. Measure the Sight: units and the building's own numbers, after "Measuring" (2015). Read the Building: the rules hidden in the architecture, after "Rules?" (2021). Still Water: drops, rain and still water through the galleries, after "water" (2007); stir the water with the pointer, tap it to play it. These three are independent homages to past exhibitions at 21_21 DESIGN SIGHT and are not affiliated with it. Room Tone is our own show about sound: seven rooms, seven instruments, and every sound drawn as it happens, with echoes traced off the model itself. Every object and text here is our own.

Sound

The sound is composed live in your browser as you move: air outside, a low hum below ground, far bells. Every exhibit object you touch makes its own sound, placed where it is in the room, with an echo shaped by that room. The narration is a synthetic voice made with the open Kokoro speech model (Apache 2.0). Press M to mute.
