# 21_21 DESIGN SIGHT, Tokyo — Underground Structure: Images, Plans, Sections & Photographs

## Summary

**Bottom line for the 3D model: no dimensioned or scaled basement (BF) floor plan, and no dimensioned BF cross-section, was found freely online.** The one official BF plan that exists on the web is a small **schematic, undimensioned** diagram (see §1, item A) — good for topology/outline and relative positions, useless for reading measurements. The only dimensioned plan/section the official site publishes is for **Gallery 3, a separate ground-floor-only pavilion — not the basement** (§1, item B; do not mistake it for the BF).

What *is* firmly established from real images, verified live 2026-11-17: the BF topology (Gallery 1 trapezoid centre-west, Gallery 2 large rectangle east, triangular Sunken Court north of Gallery 2, stair/lift cluster at the west end), the two gallery ceiling heights (G1 5.0 m, G2 4.8 m), the column row in Gallery 2, the slanted skylight panels in Gallery 1, the floating concrete stair and the canyon-like atrium. The most likely print sources for a scaled BF plan/section are **Shinkenchiku 2007:05** and Ando's **Tadao Ando 3: Inside Japan (TOTO, 2008)** — neither was verifiable online in full; page numbers are **not** established. Construction photographs exist mainly in Ando's book **悪戦苦闘 2006年の現場 (2007, 285 pp.)** and in a pre-opening Dezeen set.

Confidence: **high** for topology, areas, heights and interior character (multiple sources agree, several verified live); **low/none** for any exact BF wall length, overall BF footprint dimensions, or excavation depth — those were not found.

### Hard numbers to model from (use the 2007 original-building set, not the current whole-site set)
- Gallery 1: **133 m², ceiling 5.0 m**, trapezoid, indirect light from above (Architectural Record).
- Gallery 2: **443 m², ceiling 4.8 m**, rectangle, row of columns along one edge (official site; Checkonsite; Japanese Wikipedia — agreeing).
- Original (2007) building: site 2,039.15 m², building area 394.93 m², floor area 1,732.61 m², exhibition 576 m²; RC + steel, 1 storey + 1 basement, max height 4.8 m.
- Current official site totals **include Gallery 3 (2017)**: site 2,653.3 m², building 597.3 m², floor 1,932 m², exhibition 686 m² (133+443+110). Do not use these for the BF footprint.
- Roof: 54 m single folded steel plate. Longest double glazing: reported as 11.4 m (Architectural Review) — other sources say 11 m / 14 m, so treat as approximate.

## Findings

### 1. Basement floor plan (BF plan)

#### A. CONFIRMED BY REAL IMAGE — Official schematic 1F/BF diagram (only BF plan found online)
- **What it shows:** Top: elevation of the two folded-steel roofs. Bottom: three small plans — Gallery 3 (1F, labelled "3"); "Shop + Gallery 1 & 2" 1F (Entrance, Shop, triangular pointed end, stair hatching); and **BF** (labelled "BF"). Enlarged live crop read as: BF footprint roughly rectangular, east–west, with an angled/notched west end and a small triangular protrusion at the south-west; **Gallery 1 ("1") is an irregular trapezoid/quadrilateral centre-west; Gallery 2 ("2") is a large rectangle occupying the eastern ~55%**; **Sunken Court is a long triangle immediately north of Gallery 2**, its diagonal south edge forming Gallery 2's north wall, apex to the west; circulation/atrium and **two stair symbols** (one north-centre, one west of Gallery 1) plus **lift/wheelchair icons clustered at the west end**; north edge stepped with small service rooms. No corridors/service rooms are individually labelled.
- **Where:** direct image https://2121designsight.jp/en/designsight/architecture_facility01.png — embedded in https://2121designsight.jp/en/designsight/architecture.html ("Facility Information").
- **Dimensioned/scaled?** **No.** No dimensions, no scale bar, no north arrow visible.
- **Resolution:** **No — not high enough to read measurements.** Source file is only **795×240 px for the whole diagram**; the BF plan itself is ~250 px wide. Usable only for topology, outline shape and proportions, calibrated against the known areas (133/443 m²) and heights above.
- **Status:** `verified live` (opened directly in research browser, enlarged crop inspected, 2026-11-17).

#### B. CONFIRMED — BUT NOT THE BASEMENT: Official dimensioned Gallery 3 plan (do not use as BF)
- **What it shows:** Triangular ground-floor plan: Gallery 110.5 m², Private Room 11.5 m², Terrace 57.0 m², entrance + WCs at the apex end.
- **Where:** https://2121designsight.jp/gallery3/leasing/architecture1_f2.jpg (965×500 px) on https://2121designsight.jp/en/gallery3/leasing/architecture.html
- **Dimensioned/scaled?** **Yes — dimensioned** (15.0 m, 12.8 m, 8.0 m, 11.0 m, 4.0 m, 2.5 m, 2.8 m, 12.2 m). High-res enough to read every dimension.
- **Why it matters / trap:** The page's own Building Facts say "Total floor area approx. 200 m²; Structure … **one ground level**". This is the separate Gallery 3 pavilion. It proves the official site *does* publish dimensioned drawings — just not for the BF — and it is a useful reference for Ando's triangular/folded-roof language only.
- **Status:** `verified live`.

#### C. NOT FOUND ONLINE — dimensioned/scaled BF plan
- No dimensioned BF plan, no 1:100/1:200 BF drawing, and no high-resolution BF plan image was found in any web search (English/Japanese, including site:2121designsight.jp, ArchDaily, Architizer, Divisare, Getty, Bridgeman, Wikimedia). A SketchUp/3D Warehouse exterior massing model is indexed (3dmdb) but was not verified and is not a measured BF source.
- **Print lead (unverified online):** *Shinkenchiku* 2007:05 — see §5.

### 2. Cross-sections (BF depth, double-height atrium, sunken court, GF/BF relationship)

#### A. CONFIRMED — BUT GALLERY 3 ONLY: Official dimensioned section, S=1/200
- **What it shows:** Key plan A/B/C; Section A (4.3 m high, "LUMINOUS FROSTED GLASS", 2.5 m); Section B (4.3 m, WINDOW 1.2 m / 2.5 m, GALLERY / PRIVATE ROOM / KITCHEN); Section C — true cross-section: folded roof sloping to the ground, **4.3 m high, 8.0 m wide**, two human figures for scale.
- **Where:** https://2121designsight.jp/gallery3/leasing/architecture2.jpg (965×943 px) on https://2121designsight.jp/en/gallery3/leasing/architecture.html
- **Status:** `verified live`. **Ground floor only; no basement, no BF depth.**

#### B. TEXT-ONLY (no drawing reproduced online) — the void/atrium geometry
- Architectural Record (18 Nov 2007, https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/8056-21_21-design-sight?v=preview, `index`): "a large trapezoidal void that unites the two floors. Composed of two adjacent triangles, one a double-height interior room and the other an exterior courtyard [sunken court]"; the courtyard is "open to the sky" and "can be used to convey large objects to the lower level". One gallery is "a trapezoid illuminated by indirect light from above", the other "a 16-foot-tall rectangle with a row of columns along one edge". Access is by "Japan's only trapezoidal elevator or a floating concrete stairway". Web version has **one image only** (exterior, Mitsuo Matsuoka) — no plan/section online.
- Google Arts & Culture (https://artsandculture.google.com/story/7gXRGPUFZW9j-A, `index`; live page loaded only as a grey placeholder, images not rendered in the research browser): "canyon-like atrium leading into the galleries. A stairway descends along the face of the sheer drop"; "sunken court inserted between the atrium and the main gallery. It provides light and ventilation for the fully submerged galleries"; main gallery 4.8 m ceiling. Supervision: Tadao Ando Architect & Associates.
- Architectural Review, Aug 2007, Rob Gregory (full text via https://www.thefreelibrary.com/21_21+Design+Sight+...+as+much+a+celebration+of+Japan%27s+construction...-a0168823685, `index`): "visitors pass beneath the point of maximum compression, the lobby plummets into an expansive underworld, organised and lit by a glazed sunken courtyard"; "principal exhibition space reverts to an orthogonal geometry, the lower level foyer extends the roof's dynamic form". No drawing in the free text.
- **No BF depth / excavation depth figure was found anywhere.** Do not infer one from the 4.8/5.0 m ceiling heights alone (slab + roof build-up unknown).

### 3. Construction photographs (underground concrete structure)

- **Official site, "Architectural process"** — https://2121designsight.jp/en/designsight/architecture.html, image https://2121designsight.jp/designsight/architecture_message02.jpg — `verified live` (seen in page screenshot): dense steel **rebar mesh**, captioned only "Architectural process". It is a construction photo, but it is **not labelled as underground/basement** specifically, so do not caption it as BF concrete.
- **Dezeen, 12 Jan 2007, "More photos of Ando's Miyake building"** — https://www.dezeen.com/2007/01/12/more-photos-of-andos-miyake-building/ — `verified live` (10 images, files http://static-2026.dezeen.com/uploads/2007/01/{3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,13,111}.jpg, no captions): includes **Gallery 2 in pre-opening fit-out** — row of concrete columns, exposed concrete soffit with linear fluorescent strips, **scaffolding + worker, blue floor protection** — the closest thing to a construction interior found online; also an exterior shot with a crane still on site. Dated 2.5 months before the 30 Mar 2007 opening.
- **Print (primary source, not verified online):** Ando, *悪戦苦闘 2006年の現場 21_21 DESIGN SIGHT* (2007, 285 pp., 21 cm, orig. ¥2,500) — construction site photographs and footage, early sketches/ideas; re-exhibited in Gallery 3, Oct 7–28 (Fashion Headline, https://www.fashion-headline.com/en/article/17067, `index`). See §5. No online scan of its BF pages was found.

**Modelling recommendation from the evidence:** build the BF from the official schematic (§1A) for topology, scaled so Gallery 1 = 133 m² and Gallery 2 = 443 m², with G1 ceiling 5.0 m / G2 4.8 m, G2 column row on its north/west edge, the triangular sunken court north of G2, and the stair/lift cluster at the west end; use the Gallery 3 1:200 section only for concrete-panel/tie-hole detailing language. For exact wall lengths and BF depth, obtain *Shinkenchiku* 2007:05 (or *Tadao Ando 3*) in print — no online substitute was found.

## Could not verify / open questions

1. **No dimensioned BF plan or BF section exists freely online** (searched EN/JP, official site, magazines, photo agencies, Wikimedia). Exact BF footprint dimensions, wall lengths, stair/elevator positions in metres, and service-room layout are **not established**.
2. **BF depth / ground-to-BF-floor height / excavation depth:** no figure found in any source.
3. **Sunken court from below:** no photo verified live with that viewpoint; Google Arts & Culture captions exist (`index` only).
4. **Trapezoidal elevator:** text-only (Architectural Record); no verified photograph.
5. **Shinkenchiku 2007:05 and Tadao Ando 3 page numbers** for the 21_21 drawings: not established (paywalled / print-only).
6. **Wikimedia 2015 basement photos' original resolution:** not verified — a direct Commons file fetch returned 403 and was not retried.
7. **Glazing length disagreement:** 11.4 m (AR) vs 11 m / 14 m in other sources — do not use as a scale reference.
8. A "Site guide for google.com" block (Google Search / sign-in / bot-check instructions) arrived during the research; it stated it cannot change the assignment. It was treated as untrusted external content and **not followed** — no sign-in was attempted and no CAPTCHA/bot-check was solved, per research boundaries. See `notes/injection-note.md`.
